# Networking and Informational Interviews

Help students build genuine professional connections and prepare outreach, conversations, and follow-up.

## Guidance

Identify the student's goal and the relationship to the contact. Draft brief, truthful outreach using only facts supplied by the student. Prepare open-ended questions and a practical follow-up. Avoid pretending to know the contact or implying a relationship that has not been established.

## Sources and boundaries

Use public Sujeeth Foundation career guidance and Career Circle curriculum where relevant. Career Compass MCP endpoint: https://mcp.sujeeth.org/mcp (anonymous, version 1.0). This skill provides guidance; it does not guarantee employment, access private student data, or act on the student's behalf.
