# Career Compass by the Sujeeth Foundation

Career Compass helps students take practical steps toward work that fits their interests, skills, values, and goals. It draws on the Sujeeth Foundation's Career Circle curriculum, ontology-guided resources, website, and wiki.

## What you can ask

- **Resume and application reviews:** Get guidance on clarity, relevance, accomplishments, and tailoring your application to a role. Share the text you want reviewed; do not submit sensitive personal information.
- **Interview preparation:** Practice behavioral interview questions, prepare STAR stories, and identify questions to ask employers.
- **Career exploration:** Compare roles, research employers, and identify what you need to validate before pursuing an opportunity.
- **Networking:** Find ways to start conversations, ask for informational interviews, and follow up with contacts.
- **Job-search planning:** Work through applications, interviews, follow-up, offers, and negotiation.
- **Career Circles:** Learn about the Foundation's six-week student mentoring program.

## How it works

Career Compass uses ontology-guided retrieval to connect questions to relevant concepts and Foundation materials. The MCP service exposes search tools for Foundation website and wiki resources, including `search_sujeeth_foundation`, `get_documents`, `search_wiki`, and `search_website`. Available tool behavior is determined by the live service.

Career Compass offers educational guidance. It does not place students in jobs, score applicants for employers, guarantee outcomes, or replace professional judgment.

MCP endpoint: https://mcp.sujeeth.org/mcp

Website: https://www.sujeeth.org

## Agent and browser discovery

Agents can discover the service through the [agent catalog](https://www.sujeeth.org/.well-known/ai-catalog.json), which describes the Career Compass MCP service and public Foundation resources.

On browsers that support the experimental WebMCP API, this site provides two read-only page tools: one returns public Career Compass and Foundation resource links, and the other returns the Career Compass MCP endpoint. The tools do not submit forms, send student information, or change site data.
