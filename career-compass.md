# Career Compass

Career Compass gives students practical, source-backed guidance for preparing for a first job. Use it to work on your resume, prepare for interviews, explore roles, build professional connections, and organize a job search.

Its guidance draws on Sujeeth Foundation resources, including the Career Circle curriculum and website.

## What you can work on

- **Resumes and applications:** Clarify your experience, describe accomplishments, and tailor materials to a role. Share only the text you want reviewed; leave out sensitive personal information.
- **Interviews:** Practice behavioral questions, prepare examples from your experience, and choose useful questions to ask an employer.
- **Career exploration:** Compare roles, research employers, and identify what you need to learn before pursuing an opportunity.
- **Networking:** Start professional conversations, request informational interviews, and follow up thoughtfully.
- **Job-search planning:** Map next steps for applications, interviews, follow-up, offers, and negotiation.
- **Career Circles:** Learn about the Foundation's six-week mentoring program for college students.

## How Career Compass works

Career Compass searches Foundation resources and returns relevant sources for the question at hand. It is an educational guide, not a job-placement service or an employer screening tool. It does not submit applications, rank candidates, guarantee outcomes, or replace your judgment.

The service is available through the Model Context Protocol (MCP):

`https://mcp.sujeeth.org/mcp`

## For agents and browsers

Agents can find the service in the [agent catalog](https://www.sujeeth.org/.well-known/ai-catalog.json), which lists the public Career Compass MCP service and Foundation resources.

Browsers that support the experimental WebMCP API can access two read-only tools from this site: one returns public Career Compass and Foundation resource links; the other returns the Career Compass MCP endpoint. Neither tool submits forms, sends student information, or changes site data.
